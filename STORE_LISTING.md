# Indigo Habits — Store Listing Draft

## App name

Indigo Habits

## Subtitle / short description

Habits, affirmations, and mindful journaling

## Description

Build a more intentional daily routine with Indigo Habits.

- Track up to three habits on the free plan, with up to ten on Premium.
- Create and revisit daily affirmations; Premium removes the free custom-affirmation limit.
- Write journal entries, add photos from your library, and use your device's voice-to-text when available.
- See progress, streaks, and achievement badges.
- Set local habit and journal reminders.
- Export journal entries to PDF from the Profile screen at no additional charge.
- Restore eligible purchases from the Profile screen.

Indigo Habits uses a blue-flame indigo design.

## Premium

Premium is a subscription offered through the relevant app store. Current code gates these features: up to ten habits, unlimited custom affirmations, journal reminders, and individual habit reminders. Price and subscription terms are displayed by the store before purchase.

## Privacy summary

Core journal, habit, affirmation, progress, and reminder data are stored on the device. Voice-to-text uses operating-system speech recognition and may be processed by Apple or Google. Purchases and subscription status are handled through the app store and RevenueCat. See the hosted Privacy Policy before release.
