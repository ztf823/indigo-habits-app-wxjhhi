# Indigo Habits Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 24, 2026

This policy describes the current Indigo Habits app. A hosted copy and a working contact method must be confirmed before App Store submission.

## Information the app handles

- Journal text, habits, affirmations, progress, reminder settings, and optional profile details are stored on the device.
- If you choose a photo from your library, the app stores the selected photo reference with your profile or journal entry on the device.
- If you use voice-to-text, the app requests microphone and speech-recognition permission. Speech recognition is provided by the device operating system and may use Apple or Google services, subject to their terms and privacy policies. Indigo Habits does not use a paid AI transcription service.
- If you purchase or restore a subscription, the App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat handle purchase and subscription-status information needed to provide the entitlement.
- The app may request notification permission to schedule reminders on the device.

## How information is used

Information is used to provide journaling, habit tracking, affirmations, progress, local reminders, optional voice-to-text, PDF export, and subscription/restore functions.

## Storage, sharing, and retention

Core app content is stored locally using on-device storage. PDF export creates a file for you to share using your device's share options.

We do not sell journal content, habit data, or profile details. Information can be processed by the platform services described above when you choose to use voice-to-text or a purchase/restore flow. Optional sign-in code is present in the app; if a user elects to sign in, account credentials and account information are processed by the authentication service used for that flow.

Data remains on the device until you remove it through the app, delete the app, or otherwise clear device storage. Purchase and subscription information is retained by Apple, Google, and RevenueCat under their own policies.

## Your choices

- Do not grant photo, microphone, speech-recognition, or notification permission if you do not want to use that feature.
- Manage or revoke app permissions in device settings.
- Delete local app data through the app's data controls.
- Export journal entries to PDF from the Profile screen.
- Restore or manage subscriptions through the relevant app store.
